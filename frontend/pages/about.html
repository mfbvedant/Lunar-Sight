The Dual-Frequency Synthetic Aperture Radar (DFSAR) instrument aboard ISRO's Chandrayaan-2 orbiter operates in S-band (2.5 GHz / 12.5 cm wavelength) and L-band (1.25 GHz / 24 cm wavelength). DFSAR provides full polarimetric (quad-pol) radar backscatter measurements, enabling sub-surface penetration down to several meters inside Permanently Shadowed Regions (PSRs).

• Orbit Altitude: 100 km Circular Polar Orbit
• Slant Resolution: 2.0 to 75.0 meters/pixel
• Inc. Angles: 9.5° – 35° (Hybrid-Polarimetry R-LH, R-LV)
ICE DETECTION RADAR PHYSICS

Water ice exhibits the Coherent Backscatter Opposition Effect (CBOE), resulting in a high Circular Polarization Ratio (CPR > 1.0) and high same-sense circular backscatter. By computing Stokes parameters (S0, S1, S2, S3) and m-chi decomposition, LunarSight isolates subsurface volumetric scattering from surface roughness.

• CPR Equation: CPR = (S0 - S3) / (S0 + S3)
• Threshold Criteria: CPR > 1.25 & Low Surface Roughness
• Deep Learning Model: Weakly-Supervised U-Net (PyTorch)
