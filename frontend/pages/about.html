About LunarSight

LunarSight is a sophisticated orbital analysis platform dedicated to identifying water ice deposits within the Moon's South Pole—specifically targeting the permanently shadowed regions (PSRs). By synthesizing Chandrayaan-2 DFSAR (Dual Frequency Synthetic Aperture Radar) data with advanced neural networks, we reveal subsurface lunar volatiles hidden for billions of years.
